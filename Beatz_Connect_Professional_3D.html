Your phone is now your PC’s mouse.

Beatz Connect turns your phone into a touchpad, keyboard and media remote for Windows.

Step 1 · Pair

Enter one code

Open Beatz Connect on your PC. It shows a 6‑digit code and a QR code. Type or scan it on your phone and the two devices find each other.

Step 2 · Move

Same Wi‑Fi? It’s direct.

Drag a finger on the touchpad. Tiny movement packets go straight to your PC over the local network, with no cloud in between.

Different network

Away from home? Use the relay.

On mobile data, both devices connect out to a cloud relay over a secure port. The relay passes your movements through, so there is nothing to set up on your router.

Step 3 · Control

Your PC follows

The pointer moves at the speed you set in the desktop dashboard, which also shows your phone, battery, connection mode and ping.

Built for speed

Precise and instant

  • Pointer speed from 1.0× to 3.5×Set it once in the desktop app. Your movement is scaled before it reaches the pointer.
  • Moves like a real mouseMovement is sent as small relative steps, so the pointer never jumps.
  • Sent immediatelyEach tiny packet goes out as it happens instead of waiting in a buffer.
  • Never freezes the appTouch input at 60–120 times a second is handled on its own thread, apart from the window.
On your PC

A calm dashboard

The desktop window is a companion, like Phone Link. It shows your phone and the connection, and tucks into the tray when you close it.

Set up in four steps

  1. Install the PC app. Run the installer and finish the wizard. Beatz Connect opens its dashboard and keeps running in the system tray.
  2. Install the phone app. Open the downloaded file and allow the install.
  3. Pair. The PC shows a 6‑digit code and a QR code. Type or scan it on your phone.
  4. Control. Drag on the touchpad. The dashboard shows the status as Connected, with the mode and ping.

Questions

Windows asked about the firewall. What should I do?

Allow Beatz Connect on private networks. This lets your phone reach your PC on the same Wi‑Fi.

My phone can’t find my PC.

Check that the PC app is running in the tray and the code matches. If your phone is on mobile data or a different Wi‑Fi, use the cloud relay mode instead of the local one.

Does my movement go through the cloud?

Only on different networks. Then your movements pass through the relay server to reach your PC. On the same Wi‑Fi they go directly.

How do I remove it?

Uninstall it like any app from Windows Settings, in Apps.
